*Testing the Boop Light Detector app on an iPhone.*

Boop is an app developed for iOS users to detect levels of light and interpret this information with sound. It allows its blind and visually impaired users to detect whether lights are on or off. It covers a broad range of light levels which span from sensing daylight through open windows to checking whether the indicator light is on for a wifi router.

Boop is offered for free on the iOS store and has been downloaded over 6000 times.
