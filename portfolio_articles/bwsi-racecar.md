*Students programming small robotic cars at the MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute RACECAR course.*

The MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute is a rigorous, world-class STEM program for talented rising high school seniors. I participated as a RACECAR instructor across two summers:
* **Lead Associate Instructor (2016)**
* **Technical Instructor (2017)**

In the RACECAR course, students programmed small robotic cars (1/10th scale autonomous race cars) to autonomously navigate a racetrack, covering topics such as computer vision, visual servoing, motion planning, and obstacle avoidance.
