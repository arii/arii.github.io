*Robotic interface and experimental setup for autonomous dental crowning.*

During my time as an Undergraduate Researcher at the **Bionics Lab at UC Santa Cruz** (Summer 2010 - 2012), I worked under the advisement of Dr. Jacob Rosen on a CAD/CAM Robotic Dental Crowning Workflow. This research was supported by dual fellowships: SURF-IT and MARC.

The project focused on CAD/CAM applications in dentistry, autonomous control with mechanical systems, and UI development for robotic programs. I developed and experimentally verified a workflow to execute dental crowning and implant placement procedures on static dental models, and contributed to a system designed to implement dynamic dental procedures.
