The Lab Energy Assessment Center (LEAC) is a new organization founded from a grant won from the MIT Green Labs program. The goal of the LEAC is to work in conjunction with Green Labs and MIT Sustainability to promote resourceful energy usage by providing energy assessments free of charge to labs on campus.

As the **Lead Technology Developer**, I created scalable, open-source networking monitoring software and wireless power monitoring tools to efficiently analyze a lab's energy consumption using minimally invasive hardware.

**Awards and Grants:**
*   **2017 MIT Green Labs Innovation Award:** Won $5,000 (in collaboration with the Device Research Lab) for developing innovative technology to improve campus sustainability.
*   **2017 Earth Day Mini Grant:** Awarded for developing new fume hood technologies.
*   **2016 MIT EHS Green Labs Award:** Received $1,000 in seed funding to create green lab technology.

All software is available open-source on [GitHub](https://github.com/leac-mit).
